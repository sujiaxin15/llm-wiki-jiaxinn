# WIKI-05: Elaborative Encoding & Deep Processing

[← Back to Wiki Index](./README.md)

## Core Concept
**Elaborative encoding** enriches new information at the moment you learn it by connecting it to existing mental frameworks (schemas), vivid analogies, and personal meaning.

## Mechanism (How It Works)
Rooted in Craik & Tulving's depth-of-processing framework, semantic processing ("why/how does this work?") builds multiple associative **retrieval paths**. An isolated fact has only one fragile hook; a connected concept has many routes back to recall.

## Practical Takeaway
* **Elaborative Interrogation:** Pause after reading a concept and ask *"Why is this true?"*
* **Feynman / Beginner Translation:** Explain the idea in plain, everyday language without copying textbook phrasing.
* **Self-Reference Effect:** Link the concept to a concrete personal habit or real-life example.

## Common Myth / Trap
> **Myth:** *"Spending more hours staring at a page equals deeper learning."*  
> **Reality:** **Depth of processing**—how actively you connect meaning—matters far more than raw time spent looking at notes.
