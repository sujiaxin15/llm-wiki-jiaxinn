# WIKI-01: The Forgetting Curve & Memory Decay

[← Back to Wiki Index](./README.md)

## Core Concept
Newly learned information fades exponentially—dropping steepest within the first hours and days—unless it is actively reactivated. Forgetting is not a brain flaw; it is a natural pruning filter that clears unused traces so the brain stays efficient.

## Mechanism (How It Works)
Discovered by German psychologist Hermann Ebbinghaus (1885) using his "savings method" of relearning syllable lists, the Forgetting Curve shows that without retrieval or use, fresh synaptic traces weaken rapidly at first and then level off into a slower decline ("leaky bucket" effect).

## Practical Takeaway
Because the steepest memory drop happens within 24–72 hours of initial exposure, your first review should happen within 1 day—not weeks later right before an exam.

## Common Myth / Trap
> Myth: "I understood the lecture today, so I'll remember it next month."*  
> Reality: Understanding something in the moment (encoding) does not stop exponential decay without follow-up reactivation.
